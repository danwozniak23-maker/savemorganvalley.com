Help Us Reach Every Neighborhood

We're working to connect with every household, but we can't do it alone. If you're willing to help in your neighborhood, even for a short time, you can make a real difference.

What You'll Do

You don't need experience, just a willingness to help. You choose how much or how little you take on.

  • Deliver flyers or materials to neighbors on your street
  • Collect petition signatures from people you know
  • Show up at a park event and help sign people up
  • Be a point of contact for your block or neighborhood
  • Share our posts on social media — you can help from your couch

Even one block covered well makes a difference.

What Happens Next

After you sign up, we'll:

  • Connect you with materials
  • Help you get started in your area
  • Answer any questions

No pressure, just a chance to help your community stay informed and involved.
