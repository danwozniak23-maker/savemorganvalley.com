Help Us Reach Every Neighborhood

We're working to connect with every household, but we can't do it alone.

If you're willing to help in your neighborhood, even for a short time, you can make a real difference.

What Volunteers Do

You don't need experience, just a willingness to help.

  • Deliver materials to neighbors on your street or nearby blocks
  • Collect petition signatures from residents
  • Be a point of contact for your neighborhood

You can take on as much or as little as you're comfortable with.

Take Your Block

The most helpful volunteers are those who are willing to take responsibility for their own street or a small nearby area.

That might mean:

  • Dropping off flyers to your neighbors
  • Talking with people you already know
  • Helping make sure your street is represented

Even one block covered well makes a difference.

Flexible and Local

You choose how you want to help:

  • 20–30 minutes delivering flyers
  • Helping at a park or community event
  • Talking with neighbors when it works for you

Every bit helps us reach more people.

  • Answer any questions
