We are gathering signatures for a petition against this proposed plant. This is a physical petition - not an online form - because physical signatures carry greater weight. Each signature represents a real person, a real address, and a real voter, making it far more impactful to decision-makers than anonymous online submissions. These petitions will be submitted as part of the official record to demonstrate the concerns and position of local residents.

We, the residents and supporters of our communities, strongly oppose the proposed construction of a natural gas power plant in this area. This project threatens the health, safety, and well-being of residents, the environment, and the local economy.

Concerns are:

  • Public health and safety risks
  • Decreased property values
  • Environmental and air quality impacts
  • Increased traffic and strain on local infrastructure
  • Limited long-term economic benefit to the community

We do not support placing industrial developments like this in or near our communities. We also oppose the expansion of new natural gas power plants in the region and urge decision-makers to pursue safer, sustainable alternatives.

File a Comment with the Iowa Utilities Commission

Submitting questions or comments through the Iowa Utilities Commission's EFS ensures your concerns are part of the official record. Silence could be interpreted as agreement — make sure your voice is heard.

How to Create an Account Template Letter

To submit via the Iowa Utilities Commission's website:

  1. Visit efs.iowa.gov and click Sign Up to complete your registration.
  2. Log in to the EFS system and select File in EFS.
  3. Choose Submit a Comment or Objection in a Docket.
    • Enter Docket Number: GCU-2026-0002
    • Select your submission type
    • Explain why this plant is the wrong project and wrong location for our communities

Official comments become part of the project record.

Instructions with example submissions can be found at savemorganvalley.com or scan the QR code on our flyer.

How to Create an Account on the EFS System

  1. Open IUC Electronic Filing System website (efs.iowa.gov)
  2. Click Sign Up
  3. Fill out Registration form
  4. You will receive an email in which you will need to click on the link to "Confirm your Email Address". You may be required to log back in.
  5. You will be prompted to complete your user profile
  6. Once registration is complete, click on "File in EFS"
  7. Click on "Submit a Comment or Objection in a Docket"
  8. Select Docket No. GCU-2026-0002 and submit your comments.
  9. Finally, add your comments.

Note: For your convenience, we have provided a Template Letter (below) of opposition referencing the facts around the negative effects of having a plant like this.

Template Letter of Opposition

Use this template to submit your comment to the Iowa Utilities Commission. Fill in the blanks with your personal information.

As a resident of _______ (City), Iowa in ________County, I am asking for my complaint to be filed on Docket Number: GCU-2026-0002

Name: _________________

Address: _________________

Email Address: _________________

Phone Number: _________________

The current site for the Morgan Valley Energy Center proposed by Interstate Power and Light Company (a subsidiary of Alliant Energy) will be less than 3 miles northwest of the city; thus, it will negatively impact me personally. According to Chapter 24 of the Iowa Administrative Code, this will place my residences within the ten-mile radius of the "site impact area".

A review of historical weather data for Iowa shows that prevailing winds most frequently come from westerly quadrants — primarily W, WSW, and NW. Given this data, the residents of ________ will be disproportionately affected by the emissions from the proposed power plant. The prevailing winds coupled with the greenhouse gases and millions of tons of harmful emissions it will produce will create adverse health effects for myself and the other residents of ________.

There are several documented adverse health effects from Combustion Turbine Power Plants like the one proposed by Alliant Energy. Some of the impacts on the public include:

  • Low birth weight
  • Miscarriages
  • Premature deaths
  • Increased cases of asthma
  • Increased heart attacks
  • Increased emergency room visits
  • Increased cancer rates

Our neighboring state of Wisconsin has estimated that similar power plants will result in increased healthcare costs of over $3.5 billion. These health effects further compound healthcare costs and place strain on an already over-burdened healthcare system currently facing record high staffing shortages locally.

Additionally, it should be noted that Alliant Energy has not fulfilled the requirements outlined by Chapter 476A.6, #3. An Alliant representative stated at the Fairfax City Council meeting on March 24, 2026 that they have not "evaluated other energy options at that site" as seen on the recording at approximately 24 minutes into the recording.

Provided, the lack of compliance with Iowa Chapter 476b, the adverse health effects outlined, and the lack of positive impact on utility costs for the residents of Fairfax, I strongly urge the Office of Consumer Advocate and the Iowa Utilities Commission to deny the application for the Morgan Valley Energy Center until:

  • A location is found that will impact those individuals who will directly benefit from the reduction in their utility bills, or
  • Alternative options with less negative health impacts can be found.
