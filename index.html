SAVE MORGAN VALLEY

Stop the Gas Plant Threatening Our Communities

A large natural gas power plant is being proposed near Hwy 30 and Linn-Benton Road. It is just 1.7 miles from Atkins, 2.3 miles from Fairfax, and 2.5 miles from Cedar Rapids. Per the State of Iowa, the site impact area covers 10 miles around the proposed plant covering a large portion of both Linn and Benton Counties.

The plant would bring increased air pollution, potential health risks for our families, lower property values, and will slow the future growth of our communities. This is also at a time when cleaner, greener energy options are available.

We won't stand by. We're taking action. And you can too.

Why This Matters

Air & Health

  • Emits NOx, CO₂, and particulate matter
  • Linked to asthma and other respiratory issues
  • Impacts nearby and downwind communities most

Property & Land

  • Located on prime agricultural land
  • Industrial proximity can impact home values
  • Once developed, this land is gone permanently

Safety

  • Risk of gas leaks and industrial incidents
  • Residents deserve distance from industrial hazards

Quality of Life

  • Continuous noise and bright nighttime lighting
  • Permanent change to the rural character of the area
