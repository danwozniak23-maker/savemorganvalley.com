SAVE MORGAN VALLEY

What's Being Proposed?

A large natural gas power plant is proposed very close to Atkins, Fairfax, and Cedar Rapids.

This power plant is being built near your home.

  • A new natural gas power plant at Hwy 30 and Linn-Benton Road
  • 1.7 Miles from Atkins
  • 2.3 Miles from Fairfax
  • 2.5 Miles from Cedar Rapids
  • Per the State of Iowa, the site impact area covers 10 miles around the proposed plant

Why This Matters

Air & Health

  • Emits NOx, CO₂, and particulate matter
  • Linked to asthma and other respiratory issues
  • Impacts nearby and downwind communities most

Property & Land

  • Located on prime agricultural land
  • Industrial proximity can impact home values
  • Once developed, this land is gone permanently

Safety

  • Risk of gas leaks and industrial incidents
  • Residents deserve distance from industrial hazards

Quality of Life

  • Continuous noise and bright nighttime lighting
  • Permanent change to the rural character of the area
