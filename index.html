Public Meeting — June 24, 2026 at 6 p.m.

The Linn County Board of Supervisors will hold a public meeting to share information about the air quality permitting process related to the proposed Morgan Valley Energy Center. Staff from Linn County Public Health and Dr. Peter Thorne (UI Department of Occupational and Environmental Health) will present. The public will have an opportunity to ask questions.

Where: Jean Oxley Linn County Public Service Center – Formal Board Room, 935 2nd St. SW, Cedar Rapids

Stop the Morgan Valley Gas Plant Threatening Our Health and Livelihood

A massive 720-megawatt natural gas power plant is proposed near Highway 30 and Linn-Benton Road, directly next to our communities.

The project threatens our air quality, property values, and quality of life while creating only 4-10 permanent jobs for a plant primarily intended to support future data center growth.

Who Is Impacted?

The proposed plant site is located near the intersection of Highway 30 and Linn-Benton Road, placing multiple communities within the impact zone.

Communities by distance from the site:

  • Under 3 miles: Atkins, Cedar Rapids, Fairfax
  • 3–5 miles: Covington, Walford
  • 5+ miles: Hiawatha, Newhall, Norway, Palo, Shellsburg, Watkins

The State of Iowa designates a 10-mile impact radius around the proposed site, affecting a significant portion of both Linn and Benton Counties.

Your voice matters. Join us in protecting our communities.

Why This Matters

Air & Health

  • Emits CO₂, nitrogen oxides, and 4 other harmful pollutants
  • Linked to asthma, cancer, and other respiratory issues
  • 150-foot exhaust stacks will impact air quality

Property & Land

  • Only 4-10 permanent jobs after construction
  • No confirmed customers or power purchase agreements
  • Built to serve data centers, not local residents

Safety

  • New gas pipeline infrastructure required
  • No backup fuel sources for reliability
  • Risk of gas leaks and industrial incidents

Quality of Life

  • Uses 203 gallons per minute of Cedar Rapids water
  • Continuous noise and bright nighttime lighting
  • Permanent change to the rural character
